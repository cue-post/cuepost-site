Cuepost Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

Cuepost ("Cuepost", "we", "us") lets you choose videos from your device, set their order and posting time, and send them to your own TikTok account. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information we collect

  • TikTok account information. When you log in with TikTok, we receive your TikTok open ID and the access and refresh tokens TikTok issues to Cuepost. We request only the permissions needed to identify your account (user.info.basic) and upload videos to your TikTok drafts (video.upload).
  • Content you choose to send. The videos you select, their captions, their order, and the times you schedule. We only receive a video after you explicitly confirm in the app.
  • Technical information. Basic server logs, such as request times and error messages, used to keep the service running.

We do not access your gallery or your other media. The app can only read the items you pick.

2. How we use information

  • To log you in and keep your TikTok connection active.
  • To upload your confirmed videos to your TikTok account at the times you set.
  • To show you the status of your queued posts and to fix errors.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or add watermarks or other material to your content.

3. How long we keep information

  • Videos are stored on our servers only until they have been sent to TikTok, and are then deleted. Videos that fail to send are deleted when you remove them from your queue or request deletion.
  • Account tokens and queue records are kept while you use Cuepost, and are deleted when you disconnect your account or ask us to delete your data.

4. Sharing

We share your content with TikTok only to carry out the uploads you requested. We use hosting and storage providers to run the service, and they process data only on our behalf. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Your choices

  • Disconnect. You can revoke Cuepost's access at any time in your TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services.
  • Delete your data. Email us at the address below and we will delete your tokens, queued videos, and records.
  • Remove queued items. You can remove items from your queue before they are sent.

6. Security

We protect your data using access controls and encrypted connections. No service can guarantee complete security.

7. Children

Cuepost is not intended for anyone under 13, or under the minimum age required to use TikTok in your country. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. TikTok

Your use of TikTok is governed by TikTok's own terms and privacy policy. Cuepost is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date above when we do.
